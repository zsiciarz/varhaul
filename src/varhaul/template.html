Elements: Suhora (Kraków) eclipsing-binary ephemeris; positions and magnitudes: GCVS. Secondary minima without their own elements are at phase 0.5. σ is the timing uncertainty propagated from the elements; “?” where the ephemeris gives none.

Magnitudes are GCVS maximum–minimum in the listed band (V, p = photographic, …), so the magnitude filter mixes bands. A minimum given as a limit (“<16.”) is filtered as 16; one in brackets is an amplitude. Stars with no magnitude are hidden while a limit is set.

Azimuth runs from north (0°) through east (90°). A range whose start is larger than its end wraps through north, so 300–60 keeps the northern sky.

Depth is how far the star dims at that minimum: GCVS Min I − Max for primaries, Min II − Max (or the Min II amplitude) for secondaries. Where GCVS gives no Min II the depth is “?”, and such minima are hidden while a depth limit is set.
